# Resources for mod-107-generative-and-multimodal-eval (Generative and Multimodal Evaluation: Code, Math, RAG, Vision)

Primary sources for the material in this module. Every citation is a paper, a first-party repository, or official documentation from the benchmark or library maintainers. No secondary summaries.

## Why generation and multimodal break string match (Chapter 1)

- **Chen, M., Tworek, J., Jun, H., et al. (2021).** "Evaluating Large Language Models Trained on Code." [arXiv:2107.03374](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.03374). The Codex paper that introduced HumanEval and the pass@k unbiased estimator. §2 gives the estimator; §7 (Hazard analysis) makes the case for sandboxed execution.
- **Papineni, K., Roukos, S., Ward, T., & Zhu, W.-J. (2002).** "BLEU: a Method for Automatic Evaluation of Machine Translation." *ACL*. [aclanthology.org/P02-1040](https://aclanthology.org/P02-1040/). Background on the n-gram-overlap tradition that pass@k, RAGAS, and multimodal alignment metrics all displace for generative tasks.

## pass@k and the sandboxed execution loop (Chapter 2)

- **Chen, M., Tworek, J., Jun, H., et al. (2021).** "Evaluating Large Language Models Trained on Code" (HumanEval). [arXiv:2107.03374](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.03374). Reference for pass@k, the unbiased estimator, sandbox hazard analysis, and the HumanEval benchmark.
- **HumanEval reference implementation.** [github.com/openai/human-eval](https://github.com/openai/human-eval). The canonical `execution.py`, `evaluate_functional_correctness.py`, and the disabled-by-default `execution` gate.
- **Austin, J., Odena, A., Nye, M., Bosma, M., Michalewski, H., Dohan, D., Jiang, E., Cai, C., Terry, M., Le, Q., & Sutton, C. (2021).** "Program Synthesis with Large Language Models" (MBPP). [arXiv:2108.07732](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.07732). The MBPP benchmark.
- **Liu, J., Xia, C. S., Wang, Y., & Zhang, L. (2023).** "Is Your Code Generated by ChatGPT Really Correct? Rigorous Evaluation of Large Language Models for Code Generation" (EvalPlus / HumanEval+ / MBPP+). *NeurIPS*. [arXiv:2305.01210](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.01210). Documents the augmented-test methodology and reports the pass@1 drop from HumanEval to HumanEval+.
- **EvalPlus repository.** [github.com/evalplus/evalplus](https://github.com/evalplus/evalplus). Reference implementation for HumanEval+ / MBPP+.
- **Jain, N., Han, K., Gu, A., Li, W.-D., Yan, F., Zhang, T., Wang, S., Solar-Lezama, A., Sen, K., & Stoica, I. (2024).** "LiveCodeBench: Holistic and Contamination Free Evaluation of Large Language Models for Code." [arXiv:2403.07974](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07974). The rolling contamination-controlled code benchmark; documents the publication-date filtering methodology.
- **LiveCodeBench site and repository.** [livecodebench.github.io](https://livecodebench.github.io/) and [github.com/LiveCodeBench/LiveCodeBench](https://github.com/LiveCodeBench/LiveCodeBench).
- **Zhuo, T. Y., Vu, M. C., Chim, J., et al. (2024).** "BigCodeBench: Benchmarking Code Generation with Diverse Function Calls and Complex Instructions." [arXiv:2406.15877](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.15877). Function-completion benchmark with real library calls; substantially harder than HumanEval.
- **BigCode evaluation harness.** [github.com/bigcode-project/bigcode-evaluation-harness](https://github.com/bigcode-project/bigcode-evaluation-harness). Reference Dockerized executor used by the BigCode leaderboard.
- **Jimenez, C. E., Yang, J., Wettig, A., Yao, S., Pei, K., Press, O., & Narasimhan, K. (2024).** "SWE-bench: Can Language Models Resolve Real-World GitHub Issues?" *ICLR*. [arXiv:2310.06770](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06770). The repository-scale bug-fix benchmark referenced as the "sandbox one order of magnitude up" case.
- **SWE-bench site and Verified split.** [swebench.com](https://www.swebench.com/) and [github.com/princeton-nlp/SWE-bench](https://github.com/princeton-nlp/SWE-bench). SWE-bench Verified is the OpenAI/Princeton-curated 500-item subset with hand-verified test suites.
- **UK AISI Inspect — sandboxing documentation.** [inspect.aisi.org.uk/sandboxing.html](https://inspect.aisi.org.uk/sandboxing.html). Reference for the Docker / local sandbox primitives Inspect exposes to scorers that execute generated code.
- **Python `resource` module.** [docs.python.org/3/library/resource.html](https://docs.python.org/3/library/resource.html). Reference for `setrlimit(RLIMIT_AS, ...)`, the memory-cap primitive used in subprocess-based sandboxes.

## Math reasoning: extraction and CoT rubrics (Chapter 3)

- **Cobbe, K., Kosaraju, V., Bavarian, M., Chen, M., Jun, H., Kaiser, L., Plappert, M., Tworek, J., Hilton, J., Nakano, R., Hesse, C., & Schulman, J. (2021).** "Training Verifiers to Solve Math Word Problems" (GSM8K). [arXiv:2110.14168](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.14168). The GSM8K benchmark and the `#### N` answer-format convention.
- **GSM8K dataset repository.** [github.com/openai/grade-school-math](https://github.com/openai/grade-school-math). Reference dataset and evaluation script.
- **Hendrycks, D., Burns, C., Kadavath, S., Arora, A., Basart, S., Tang, E., Song, D., & Steinhardt, J. (2021).** "Measuring Mathematical Problem Solving with the MATH Dataset." *NeurIPS Datasets & Benchmarks*. [arXiv:2103.03874](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.03874). The MATH benchmark; the `\boxed{}` convention and the `math_equivalence.py` normaliser.
- **MATH dataset repository.** [github.com/hendrycks/math](https://github.com/hendrycks/math). Reference dataset and evaluation utilities.
- **Lewkowycz, A., Andreassen, A., Dohan, D., Dyer, E., Michalewski, H., Ramasesh, V., Slone, A., Anil, C., Schlag, I., Gutman-Solo, T., Wu, Y., Neyshabur, B., Gur-Ari, G., & Misra, V. (2022).** "Solving Quantitative Reasoning Problems with Language Models" (Minerva). *NeurIPS*. [arXiv:2206.14858](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.14858). Contributes the improved MATH normalisation pipeline that later harnesses folded in.
- **Wang, X., Wei, J., Schuurmans, D., Le, Q., Chi, E., Narang, S., Chowdhery, A., & Zhou, D. (2022).** "Self-Consistency Improves Chain of Thought Reasoning in Language Models." *ICLR 2023*. [arXiv:2203.11171](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.11171). Defines `acc@maj-N` and shows the lift over greedy on GSM8K / MATH.
- **Wei, J., Wang, X., Schuurmans, D., Bosma, M., Ichter, B., Xia, F., Chi, E. H., Le, Q., & Zhou, D. (2022).** "Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models." *NeurIPS*. [arXiv:2201.11903](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.11903). The chain-of-thought prompting paper; background for the rubric-scored process eval.
- **Uesato, J., Kushman, N., Kumar, R., Song, F., Siegel, N., Wang, L., Creswell, A., Irving, G., & Higgins, I. (2022).** "Solving math word problems with process- and outcome-based feedback." [arXiv:2211.14275](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.14275). Formalises process-vs-outcome supervision for math reasoning.
- **Lightman, H., Kosaraju, V., Burda, Y., Edwards, H., Baker, B., Lee, T., Leike, J., Schulman, J., Sutskever, I., & Cobbe, K. (2023).** "Let's Verify Step by Step." [arXiv:2305.20050](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.20050). Introduces PRM800K, the reference step-level correctness dataset; the reference for step-level CoT rubrics.
- **PRM800K dataset.** [github.com/openai/prm800k](https://github.com/openai/prm800k). The step-level labels released with Lightman et al. 2023.
- **Zhang, H., Da, J., Lee, D., Robinson, V., Wu, C., Song, W., Zhao, T., Raja, P., Slack, D., Lyu, Q., Hendryx, S., Kaplan, R., Lunati, M., & Yue, S. (2024).** "A Careful Examination of Large Language Model Performance on Grade School Arithmetic." *NeurIPS*. [arXiv:2405.00332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00332). Introduces GSM1k, a fresh matched-difficulty benchmark for measuring GSM8K contamination. The reference for the contamination discussion in Chapter 3.
- **SymPy.** [sympy.org](https://www.sympy.org/) and [docs.sympy.org](https://docs.sympy.org/latest/index.html). Reference for `sympify`, `parse_latex`, and `simplify`, used in the CAS-equivalence layer for MATH.
- **lm-evaluation-harness — GSM8K task.** [github.com/EleutherAI/lm-evaluation-harness/tree/main/lm_eval/tasks/gsm8k](https://github.com/EleutherAI/lm-evaluation-harness/tree/main/lm_eval/tasks/gsm8k). Reference task YAML with the extraction filter chain.
- **lm-evaluation-harness — MATH tasks.** [github.com/EleutherAI/lm-evaluation-harness/tree/main/lm_eval/tasks/hendrycks_math](https://github.com/EleutherAI/lm-evaluation-harness/tree/main/lm_eval/tasks/hendrycks_math) and the Minerva-style variant under `minerva_math/`.

## RAG metrics with RAGAS and TruLens (Chapter 4)

- **Es, S., James, J., Espinosa-Anke, L., & Schockaert, S. (2023).** "RAGAS: Automated Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation." *EACL 2024 (Demo)*. [arXiv:2309.15217](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.15217). The RAGAS paper. Defines faithfulness, answer relevancy, context precision, and context recall.
- **RAGAS documentation.** [docs.ragas.io](https://docs.ragas.io/). Reference for the current metric surface, judge configuration, and integration with LangChain / LlamaIndex output shapes.
- **RAGAS repository.** [github.com/explodinggradients/ragas](https://github.com/explodinggradients/ragas). Reference implementation.
- **TruLens documentation.** [trulens.org](https://www.trulens.org/) and [github.com/truera/trulens](https://github.com/truera/trulens). Reference for the RAG Triad (context relevance, groundedness, answer relevance), feedback functions, and trace-based instrumentation.
- **TruLens — RAG Triad guide.** [trulens.org/getting_started/core_concepts/rag_triad](https://www.trulens.org/getting_started/core_concepts/rag_triad/). The authoritative statement of the triad framing.
- **Lewis, P., Perez, E., Piktus, A., Petroni, F., Karpukhin, V., Goyal, N., Küttler, H., Lewis, M., Yih, W.-t., Rocktäschel, T., Riedel, S., & Kiela, D. (2020).** "Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks." *NeurIPS*. [arXiv:2005.11401](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.11401). The original RAG paper; background for framing the pipeline as an instrumentation surface.
- **Karpukhin, V., Oğuz, B., Min, S., Lewis, P., Wu, L., Edunov, S., Chen, D., & Yih, W.-t. (2020).** "Dense Passage Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering." *EMNLP*. [arXiv:2004.04906](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.04906). Background for embedding-based retrieval assumed by the pipeline in exercise-03.
- **HELM — RAG scenarios.** [crfm.stanford.edu/helm/](https://crfm.stanford.edu/helm/). HELM provides RAG-adjacent scenarios (NaturalQuestions, HotpotQA) with reproducible evaluation methodology; useful as a comparison harness to RAGAS/TruLens.

## RAG failure modes (Chapter 5)

- **Es, S., James, J., Espinosa-Anke, L., & Schockaert, S. (2023).** "RAGAS." [arXiv:2309.15217](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.15217). The paper's own limitations discussion covers the omission asymmetry in faithfulness and the reference-vs-reference-free tradeoff.
- **Zheng, L., Chiang, W.-L., Sheng, Y., et al. (2023).** "Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena." *NeurIPS Datasets & Benchmarks*. [arXiv:2306.05685](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685). Documents position bias, verbosity bias, and self-preference in LLM judges — all of which propagate into RAGAS/TruLens metrics.
- **Panickssery, A., Bowman, S. R., & Feng, S. (2024).** "LLM Evaluators Recognize and Favor Their Own Generations." [arXiv:2404.13076](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.13076). The self-preference paper; the reference for "do not use the same model as generator and judge" in RAG eval.
- **Saad-Falcon, J., Khattab, O., Potts, C., & Zaharia, M. (2024).** "ARES: An Automated Evaluation Framework for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems." *NAACL*. [arXiv:2311.09476](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09476). Alternative RAG evaluation framework; useful counterpoint to RAGAS on the judge-training methodology.
- **Chen, J., Lin, H., Han, X., & Sun, L. (2024).** "Benchmarking Large Language Models in Retrieval-Augmented Generation" (RGB). *AAAI*. [arXiv:2309.01431](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.01431). A RAG benchmark that explicitly probes noise-robustness, negative rejection (refusal), information integration, and counterfactual robustness — the axes the RAGAS core metrics don't cover.
- **Cohen, J. (1960).** "A Coefficient of Agreement for Nominal Scales." *Educational and Psychological Measurement*, 20(1), 37–46. Cohen's κ; the reference for the judge-vs-human calibration protocol in exercise-03.
- **Landis, J. R., & Koch, G. G. (1977).** "The Measurement of Observer Agreement for Categorical Data." *Biometrics*, 33(1), 159–174. κ interpretation thresholds used as the acceptance criterion.

## Multimodal eval: VQA, alignment, and modality coverage (Chapter 6)

- **Singh, A., Natarajan, V., Shah, M., Jiang, Y., Chen, X., Batra, D., Parikh, D., & Rohrbach, M. (2019).** "Towards VQA Models That Can Read" (TextVQA). *CVPR*. [arXiv:1904.08920](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.08920). VQA with reading-text-in-image as a required capability.

- **MMMU site.** [mmmu-benchmark.github.io](https://mmmu-benchmark.github.io/).
- **Liu, Y., Duan, H., Zhang, Y., Li, B., Zhang, S., Zhao, W., Yuan, Y., Wang, J., He, C., Liu, Z., Chen, K., & Lin, D. - **Mathew, M., Karatzas, D., & Jawahar, C. V. (2021).** "DocVQA: A Dataset for VQA on Document Images." *WACV*. [arXiv:2007.00398](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.00398). Document-layout VQA benchmark.

- **LMMs-Eval — multimodal evaluation harness.** [github.com/EvolvingLMMs-Lab/lmms-eval](https://github.com/EvolvingLMMs-Lab/lmms-eval). The most active multimodal-eval harness; a fork of lm-evaluation-harness with VLM-native adapters.

## Composing a product-shaped eval suite (Chapter 7)

- **Mitchell, M., Wu, S., Zaldivar, A., Barnes, P., Vasserman, L., Hutchinson, B., Spitzer, E., Raji, I. D., & Gebru, T. (2019).** "Model Cards for Model Reporting." *FAT\**. [arXiv:1810.03993](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.03993). The model-card standard the "leaderboard collage" section critiques as a starting template, not an ending one.
- **Gebru, T., Morgenstern, J., Vecchione, B., Vaughan, J. W., Wallach, H., Daumé III, H., & Crawford, K. (2021).** "Datasheets for Datasets." *Communications of the ACM*, 64(12), 86–92. [arXiv:1803.09010](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09010). The dataset-documentation standard referenced by the reproducibility manifest.
- **Reuel, A., Hardy, A., Smith, C., Lamparth, M., Hardy, M., & Kochenderfer, M. J. (2024).** "BetterBench: Assessing AI Benchmarks, Uncovering Issues, and Establishing Best Practices." *NeurIPS Datasets & Benchmarks*. [arXiv:2411.12990](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.12990). Recent survey; the checklist overlaps with the Chapter 7 writeup template.
- **Bowman, S. R., & Dahl, G. E. (2021).** "What Will it Take to Fix Benchmarking in Natural Language Understanding?" *NAACL*. [arXiv:2104.02145](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.02145). Motivates the "your suite is a construction, not a leaderboard column" argument.
- **NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0), 2023.** [nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework). The "Measure" function references reproducible evaluation and coverage-gap disclosure as governance requirements.
- **Liang, P., Bommasani, R., Lee, T., et al. (2022).** "Holistic Evaluation of Language Models" (HELM). [arXiv:2211.09110](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.09110). HELM's *scenario × metric* matrix framing is the prior-art version of the capability map — useful as a comparison to the product-shaped composition pattern.
