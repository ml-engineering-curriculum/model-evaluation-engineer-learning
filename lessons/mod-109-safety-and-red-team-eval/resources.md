# Resources for mod-109-safety-and-red-team-eval (Safety and Red-Team Evaluation)

Primary sources for the material in this module. Every citation is a paper, a first-party repository, official documentation from a benchmark maintainer, or a public governance-framework document. No secondary summaries.

## Why safety eval is its own discipline (Chapter 1)

- **Mitchell, M., Wu, S., Zaldivar, A., Barnes, P., Vasserman, L., Hutchinson, B., Spitzer, E., Raji, I. D., & Gebru, T. (2019).** "Model Cards for Model Reporting." *FAT\**. [arXiv:1810.03993](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.03993). The reference the Chapter 1 dashboard framing and the Chapter 7 model-card skeleton descend from.
- **OpenAI (2023).** "GPT-4 System Card." [cdn.openai.com/papers/gpt-4-system-card.pdf](https://cdn.openai.com/papers/gpt-4-system-card.pdf). Reference for the aggregate-in-public, controlled-detail-internal pattern that Chapter 1 draws as Boundary 2.
- **Anthropic (2024).** "Claude 3 Model Card." [www-cdn.anthropic.com/de8ba9b01c9ab7cbabf5c33b80b7bbc618857627/Model_Card_Claude_3.pdf](https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/de8ba9b01c9ab7cbabf5c33b80b7bbc618857627/Model_Card_Claude_3.pdf). A safety-section reference against which the Chapter 7 skeleton is compared.
- **Meta (2024).** "The Llama 3 Herd of Models." [arXiv:2407.21783](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21783). Reference for the safety-eval structure in the Llama 3 / 3.1 reports; §5 of the paper documents the model-card-level safety methodology.
- **Google (2023).** "Gemini: A Family of Highly Capable Multimodal Models." [arXiv:2312.11805](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.11805). Reference for the Gemini technical report's safety section shape.
- **NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0), 2023.** [nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework). Backstop for the "safety eval is governance-adjacent" framing; the "Measure" function names the evaluate-map-manage discipline this module operationalizes.

## Refusal and over-refusal (Chapter 2)

- **Röttger, P., Kirk, H. R., Vidgen, B., Attanasio, G., Bianchi, F., & Hovy, D. (2024).** "XSTest: A Test Suite for Identifying Exaggerated Safety Behaviours in Large Language Models." *NAACL*. [arXiv:2308.01263](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.01263). The canonical benign-lookalike over-refusal benchmark; source of the "how do I kill a Python process" style examples and the ~250-item test set.
- **Cui, J., Chiang, W.-L., Stoica, I., & Hsieh, C.-J. (2024).** "OR-Bench: An Over-Refusal Benchmark for Large Language Models." [arXiv:2405.20947](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.20947). Second-generation over-refusal benchmark; used as the augmenting source in exercise-01.
- **Mazeika, M., Phan, L., Yin, X., Zou, A., Wang, Z., Mu, N., Sakhaee, E., Li, N., Basart, S., Li, B., Forsyth, D., & Hendrycks, D. (2024).** "HarmBench: A Standardized Evaluation Framework for Automated Red Teaming and Robust Refusal." *ICML*. [arXiv:2402.04249](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04249). The `standard` split provides the harmful-request side of the paired Chapter 2 measurement.
- **Inan, H., Upasani, K., Chi, J., Rungta, R., Iyer, K., Mao, Y., Tontchev, M., Hu, Q., Fuller, B., Testuggine, D., & Khabsa, M. (2023).** "Llama Guard: LLM-based Input-Output Safeguard for Human-AI Conversations." [arXiv:2312.06674](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06674). Reference for the trained-classifier compliance judge option; Llama-Guard-2 / -3 follow-ons at [huggingface.co/meta-llama](https://huggingface.co/meta-llama) as the current open reference.
- **Meta (2024).** "Meta Llama Guard 3." [huggingface.co/meta-llama/Llama-Guard-3-8B](https://huggingface.co/meta-llama/Llama-Guard-3-8B). Model card for the current open reference trained-classifier judge cited in exercise-01.
- **Wilson, E. B. (1927).** "Probable Inference, the Law of Succession, and Statistical Inference." *Journal of the American Statistical Association*, 22(158), 209–212. Reference for the Wilson interval used as a closed-form proportion CI when bootstrap is not warranted.
- **Efron, B., & Tibshirani, R. J. (1993).** *An Introduction to the Bootstrap.* Chapman & Hall. Reference the paired-bootstrap CI in Chapter 2 §"paired confidence interval" descends from.
- **OpenAI Moderation API — documentation.** [platform.openai.com/docs/guides/moderation](https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/moderation). Reference for the moderation category taxonomy cited as a reference schema for harmonizing policy categories across labs.

## Jailbreak resistance with HarmBench (Chapter 3)

- **Mazeika, M., Phan, L., Yin, X., Zou, A., Wang, Z., Mu, N., Sakhaee, E., Li, N., Basart, S., Li, B., Forsyth, D., & Hendrycks, D. (2024).** "HarmBench: A Standardized Evaluation Framework for Automated Red Teaming and Robust Refusal." *ICML*. [arXiv:2402.04249](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04249). The Chapter 3 instrument. Defines behaviors, splits (`standard`, `contextual`), the `attacks/` module, and the trained compliance classifier.
- **HarmBench repository.** [github.com/centerforaisafety/HarmBench](https://github.com/centerforaisafety/HarmBench). Reference implementation, attacker methods, and classifier weights (`cais/HarmBench-Llama-2-13b-cls`, later revisions on HF Hub).
- **Chao, P., Robey, A., Dobriban, E., Hassani, H., Pappas, G. J., & Wong, E. (2023).** "Jailbreaking Black Box Large Language Models in Twenty Queries" (PAIR). [arXiv:2310.08419](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08419). The PAIR iterative attacker referenced as the automated-attacker option in Chapter 3 and exercise-02.
- **Mehrotra, A., Zampetakis, M., Kassianik, P., Nelson, B., Anderson, H., Singer, Y., & Karbasi, A. (2024).** "Tree of Attacks: Jailbreaking Black-Box LLMs Automatically" (TAP). [arXiv:2312.02119](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.02119). The TAP iterative attacker; the tree-of-attacks generalization of PAIR.
- **Russinovich, M., Salem, A., & Eldan, R. (2024).** "Great, Now Write an Article About That: The Crescendo Multi-Turn LLM Jailbreak Attack." [arXiv:2404.01833](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01833). The Crescendo multi-turn attacker; the reference for the "attacker uses several turns to soften refusal" pattern named in Chapter 3.
- **Zou, A., Wang, Z., Kolter, J. Z., & Fredrikson, M. (2023).** "Universal and Transferable Adversarial Attacks on Aligned Language Models" (GCG + AdvBench). [arXiv:2307.15043](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.15043). Reference for the GCG suffix-optimization attacker and the AdvBench corpus cited as a comparable public suite.
- **Chao, P., Debenedetti, E., Robey, A., Andriushchenko, M., Croce, F., Sehwag, V., Dobriban, E., Flammarion, N., Pappas, G. J., Tramèr, F., Hassani, H., & Wong, E. (2024).** "JailbreakBench: An Open Robustness Benchmark for Jailbreaking Large Language Models." *NeurIPS Datasets & Benchmarks*. [arXiv:2404.01318](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01318). Reference for the JailbreakBench suite cited as a comparable published corpus.
- **Andriushchenko, M., Croce, F., & Flammarion, N. (2024).** "Jailbreaking Leading Safety-Aligned LLMs with Simple Adaptive Attacks." [arXiv:2404.02151](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.02151). Reference for the Best-of-N and simple-adaptive-attacker family cited in Chapter 3's attacker taxonomy. <!-- needs-research: the "Best-of-N Jailbreaking" paper (Hughes et al. 2024, Anthropic) is a separate and directly on-topic citation; confirm which is the intended Chapter 3 anchor and add the other if both are meant. -->

## Bias, toxicity, and fairness at the model level (Chapter 5)

- **Parrish, A., Chen, A., Nangia, N., Padmakumar, V., Phang, J., Thompson, J., Htut, P. M., & Bowman, S. R. (2022).** "BBQ: A Hand-Built Bias Benchmark for Question Answering." *Findings of ACL*. [arXiv:2110.08193](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.08193). The bias-in-QA benchmark; 11 categories, ambiguous / disambiguated split, signed bias-score formula.
- **BBQ repository.** [github.com/nyu-mll/BBQ](https://github.com/nyu-mll/BBQ). Reference dataset load and per-category evaluators.
- **Nadeem, M., Bethke, A., & Reddy, S. (2021).** "StereoSet: Measuring Stereotypical Bias in Pretrained Language Models." *ACL*. [arXiv:2004.09456](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.09456). Cited alongside BBQ as the stereotype-in-completion benchmark.
- **Zhao, J., Wang, T., Yatskar, M., Ordonez, V., & Chang, K.-W. (2018).** "Gender Bias in Coreference Resolution: Evaluation and Debiasing Methods" (WinoBias). *NAACL*. [arXiv:1804.06876](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.06876). The occupational-stereotype benchmark cited in Chapter 5.
- **Rudinger, R., Naradowsky, J., Leonard, B., & Van Durme, B. (2018).** "Gender Bias in Coreference Resolution" (WinoGender). *NAACL*. [arXiv:1804.09301](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.09301). Sister benchmark to WinoBias.
- **Gehman, S., Gururangan, S., Sap, M., Choi, Y., & Smith, N. A. (2020).** "RealToxicityPrompts: Evaluating Neural Toxic Degeneration in Language Models." *Findings of EMNLP*. [arXiv:2009.11462](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.11462). Defines the 100k prompt set, the toxic / non-toxic splits, and the EMT and toxicity-probability metrics used in exercise-04.
- **Hartvigsen, T., Gabriel, S., Palangi, H., Sap, M., Ray, D., & Kamar, E. (2022).** "ToxiGen: A Large-Scale Machine-Generated Dataset for Adversarial and Implicit Hate Speech Detection." *ACL*. [arXiv:2203.09509](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.09509). Reference for the ToxiGen-trained classifier cited as one of the two-or-more classifiers in exercise-04.
- **Detoxify (unitary/detoxify).** [github.com/unitaryai/detoxify](https://github.com/unitaryai/detoxify). Reference open-source toxicity classifier used in exercise-04.
- **Perspective API — Jigsaw.** [perspectiveapi.com](https://perspectiveapi.com/) and [developers.perspectiveapi.com](https://developers.perspectiveapi.com/). Reference for the Perspective classifier cited throughout Chapter 5 as the industry-standard toxicity scorer.
- **Sap, M., Card, D., Gabriel, S., Choi, Y., & Smith, N. A. (2019).** "The Risk of Racial Bias in Hate Speech Detection." *ACL*. [aclanthology.org/P19-1163](https://aclanthology.org/P19-1163/). The load-bearing reference for AAVE false-positive elevation in Perspective and comparable classifiers. Cited in exercise-04's FPR audit.
- **Davani, A. M., Díaz, M., & Prabhakaran, V. (2022).** "Dealing with Disagreements: Looking Beyond the Majority Vote in Subjective Annotations." *TACL*. [arXiv:2110.05719](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.05719). Rater-disagreement reference for Chapter 5 §"Rater bias in the source data."
- **Dhamala, J., Sun, T., Kumar, V., Krishna, S., Pruksachatkun, Y., Chang, K.-W., & Gupta, R. (2021).** "BOLD: Dataset and Metrics for Measuring Biases in Open-Ended Language Generation" (FAccT). [arXiv:2101.11718](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.11718). The stereotype-in-completion benchmark for the fairness-slice measurements.
- **Benjamini, Y., & Hochberg, Y. (1995).** "Controlling the False Discovery Rate: A Practical and Powerful Approach to Multiple Testing." *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series B*, 57(1), 289–300. Reference for the BH FDR correction used across BBQ categories, toxicity slices, and demographic axes.
