## What changed since the 2026-09 cycle

The 90-day window shifted by one month (2026-06-05→2026-09-03 → 2026-07-05→2026-10-03). The market moved in ways that reinforce — not destabilize — the existing 12-module curriculum, but there are several notable shifts worth logging:

- **Sample grew from 31 to 36 postings.** Twelve prior-cycle postings dropped (window / listing / seniority rules); seventeen net-new postings entered — a 25% employer-base expansion driven primarily by the aggregator-tail and new-frontier-lab segments.
- **Three new frontier-lab entrants:** Thinking Machines Lab (Software Engineer, Evaluation Platform / Infra, $300K–$350K), Reflection AI (MoTS Research SWE, Safety Evaluations Infrastructure, CBRN gating for open-model release decisions), and Mistral AI (Evaluation Engineer, Applied AI, Paris). Each explicitly scopes eval-platform engineering as a core role shape — direct evidence that `req-eval-platform-engineering` (0.72 this cycle, up from 0.68) is now a titled job family across frontier labs, not just a frontier-lab-of-choice workload. All three absorb cleanly into [`mod-111`](lessons/mod-111-eval-platform-engineering) and [`project-103`](projects/project-103-eval-platform-capstone).
- **Microsoft AI health vertical doubles down:** `microsoft-mee-01` (NYC Mayo Clinic embed) is joined by `microsoft-mee-02` (London Applied AI SWE, Health). Two in-window health-eval postings from the same employer in different geographies is the strongest single-cycle signal that domain-specialized eval is becoming a geographically distributed IC seat family. Both covered by [`mod-112`](lessons/mod-112-eval-systems-design) (HIPAA / clinical validation framing) and `req-multimodal-rag-eval` (mod-107) for the agentic/trajectory evaluation angle.
- **First regulated-finance IC eval seat observed:** Capital One `capitalone-mee-01` ("AI Engineer 4 (AI Foundations: Benchmarking, Evaluation, and Explainability)," McLean VA, 2026-10-01) — the first time a regulated-finance employer has published an IC-level eval-engineer-shaped posting that fuses benchmarking + evaluation + explainability + model governance (producibility, lineage tracking, automated retraining schedules) in a single seat. Covered by [`mod-112`](lessons/mod-112-eval-systems-design) + [`mod-102`](lessons/mod-102-benchmark-engineering).
- **Apollo Research further splits its eval-SWE lane:** last cycle Apollo split research-heavy vs SWE-execution; this cycle the SWE-execution lane split further into Full-stack (apollo-mee-02) and Backend (apollo-mee-03). Apollo is now hiring three parallel eval-engineer-shaped roles. Explicitly declares Inspect as primary evals framework.
- **METR broadens from one to three eval-engineer-shaped roles:** `metr-mee-01` (MTS Evaluation Execution, salary band widened to $328K–$579K) remains the operations lane; `metr-mee-02` (NEW this cycle: Task Development Engineer, $261K–$385K) explicitly names RE-Bench, HCAST, SWE-bench Verified, Cybench, GPQA, Inspect, Hawk, and Time Horizons — the single most benchmark-literacy-dense JD in the sample.

### Reading the evidence-frequency numbers

- **Denominator = 36** (postings sampled this cycle). The window closed on 2026-10-03; if a requirement is not cited by ≥30% (≥11/36) of postings, the packet does not authorise net-new curriculum content for it, even if the requirement is widely accepted as important. See [`.aicg/curriculum-plan-delta.json`](.aicg/curriculum-plan-delta.json) for the empty-arrays proposal.
- **Low-frequency but essential-foundation requirements** (mod-103 classical eval at 0.14; mod-106 human eval at 0.14) are retained at full depth because they underlie higher-frequency work. Postings sample the surface (what the JD asks about); the curriculum teaches the foundation and the surface. Do not demote without cross-referencing peer-track posting evidence.
- **Highest-frequency requirements this cycle**: eval platform engineering (0.72 — up from 0.68), agent & tool eval (0.61 — flat), benchmark engineering (0.56 — flat), evaluation methodology (0.50 — up from 0.48), safety/red-team (0.33 — down from 0.39), production regression (0.33 — flat), LLM benchmark harnesses (0.31 — up from 0.26, at threshold but fully covered).
- **Denominator changed vs last cycle** (was 31, now 36). Direct year-over-year frequency comparisons should divide the deltas by roughly `(current/36 - previous/31)`, not treat 0.01–0.03 movements as material.

## Ownership map — quick reference

- **Model Evaluation Engineer (this track, level 30)** owns evaluation engineering end-to-end at depth: validity & statistical methodology → benchmark engineering → harnesses across modalities (classical, LLM, multimodal, agentic, voice) → human / LLM-judge calibration → safety & red-team measurement → production regression detection → eval platform engineering → release-gate systems design. Cross-modality and platform-altitude remain the differentiators against peer tracks.
- **ML Engineer** (level 20) owns the build-altitude practitioner workflow that this curriculum assumes.
- **AI Eval Engineer** (level 25, AI Engineering family) is the **peer** that owns build-altitude LLM/agent eval-harness work inside product pipelines.
- **AI Evaluation Engineer** (level 25, Governance family) is the **peer** that owns release-assurance / governance shape (audit trails, regulator-facing documentation, third-party evaluator interface).
- **Fine-Tuning Engineer** (level 30) is the **peer specialist** that produces the models this curriculum evaluates.
- **AI Risk Engineer** (level 30) is the **peer specialist** that designs harm models and generates red-team data; this curriculum *measures* safety properties from that input.
- **Senior ML Engineer** (level 30, peer ladder) is the generalist peer at the same level.
- **Staff ML Engineer** (level 40) and higher inherit / link to this packet for evaluation depth.

## Differentiation versus the three peer "eval" tracks

| Track | Family | Owns | Posting shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| `model-evaluation-engineer` (this, level 30) | ML Engineering | Benchmark engineering depth, statistical methodology, multi-modality (classical / LLM / multimodal / agent / voice), eval platform architecture | The 36 postings in this packet |
| `ai-eval-engineer` (level 25) | AI Engineering | Build-altitude LLM/agent harness practitioner work inside product apps (trajectory eval, eval-gated CI, eval dashboards for app teams) | App-embedded eval postings |
| `ai-evaluation-engineer` (level 25) | Governance | Release-assurance / governance shape — audit trails, regulator-facing docs, third-party evaluator interface | Regulator-facing / QMS postings |

If a posting emphasises **benchmark construction, statistical rigour, eval platform internals, or cross-modality measurement at depth**, route it here. If it emphasises **product-side eval pipelines, prompt CI, or app-team enablement**, route it to `ai-eval-engineer`. If it emphasises **audit trails, regulator interface, or release-governance evidence**, route it to `ai-evaluation-engineer`.
